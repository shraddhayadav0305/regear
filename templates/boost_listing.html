Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after the boost period ends?
Your listing will continue to be visible but with regular (non-boosted) visibility. You can boost it again anytime to regain high visibility.
Can I get a refund if I don't like the results?
We offer a 48-hour satisfaction guarantee. If you're not satisfied with the visibility boost, contact our support team for a refund.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets. All transactions are 100% secure and encrypted.
How does homepage feature work?
Your product will be displayed prominently on the ReGear homepage, in the featured products carousel. This gives you maximum visibility among all users.
Can I cancel a boost anytime?
Boosts cannot be cancelled once purchased, but you won't be charged again. The boost period will run for the full duration you selected.
