## 📱 Getting Started (New User)

### Step 1: Register
Visit **`/register`** and fill in:
- Full Name (your real name)
- Email Address
- Phone Number (10-15 digits)
- Password (min 6 characters)
- Confirm Password

**Takes 2 minutes!** No credit card needed.

### Step 2: Your Dashboard
After login, you'll see `/dashboard` with:
- **Active Listings** - Products that are currently live
- **Expired Listings** - Products past 5 days that didn't sell
- **Sold Items** - Successfully sold products
- Quick stats on sales and ratings

### Step 3: Post Your First Product
Click **"Post New Product"** button or go to `/sell`

**Simple 3-step process:**
1. Select category & subcategory
2. Fill product details:
   - Title (e.g., "iPhone 13 Pro 256GB")
   - Description (tell buyers about condition, features)
   - Price in ₹
   - Condition (New/Used)
   - Location
   - Upload 1-5 product images
3. Click "Post Product" → **It's LIVE immediately!** 🎉

**That's it!** Your product is now visible to all buyers for 5 days.

## ✅ How to Mark Product as SOLD

When you successfully sell a product:
1. Go to `/my-listings` or dashboard
2. Find the product in "Active Listings"
3. Click **"Mark Sold"** button
4. Confirm

✓ Product will be labeled "SOLD"
✓ Your completed_sales counter increases
✓ You lose the trial cost (₹0 anyway!)

## 🚀 Pro Tips for Fast Sales

### 1. Great Product Images
- Take clear, well-lit photos
- Show product from multiple angles
- Include any defects (builds trust!)
- Don't use blur or filters

### 2. Honest Descriptions
- Describe condition accurately
- Mention any scratches/dents
- List all features/specs
- Don't oversell

### 3. Competitive Pricing
- Research similar products
- Price slightly below market
- Be open to reasonable offers

### 4. Quick Responses
- Respond to inquiries within hours
- Be polite and professional
- Answer all questions

### 5. Use Boosts Smartly
- If product is popular but not selling → Boost!
- Premium items → Try Featured boost
- Common items → Start with Basic boost
